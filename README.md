#Introduction😎😎

EYESEE-AI is an explainable artificial intelligence platform designed to support early screening of Diabetic Retinopathy, especially in rural and low-connectivity healthcare settings. The system combines retinal image analysis with Grad-CAM explainability, allowing users to see not only the screening result but also the areas of the retinal image that influenced the prediction.

The platform provides an end-to-end workflow covering patient registration, retinal image capture or upload, AI-based screening, explainable results, referral recommendations, and screening history. It is designed with an offline-first and multilingual approach to make the solution more accessible and practical for frontline healthcare workers.

The goal of EYESEE-AI is to make diabetic retinopathy screening more accessible, understandable, and efficient, while supporting healthcare professionals rather than replacing clinical diagnosis.
